# Learning route

The single educational anchor is **John Watrous, *The Theory of Quantum Information* (2018)**. The [source bridge](watrous_bridge.md) begins with two excited states that decay at different rates, then connects their lost coherence to the first-event record, finite clock and complete simulation theorem. It identifies verified textbook sections and distinguishes their foundations from the project's derivations and imported algorithmic primitives.

The [30-milestone map](milestones.md) contains 12 background/preliminaries milestones and 18 narrative/innovation milestones. The first phase builds the state, channel, reference, access and error framework. The second assembles unequal-rate coherence, the waiting-time compiler, its full resource bound and its limits. Each milestone has an understanding target, earlier dependencies and source pointers.

Use these two entry points together:

1. Read the [bridge's worked example](watrous_bridge.md#unequal-rates-make-the-record-visible) to see why populations alone are insufficient.
2. Follow the [milestone map](milestones.md) from its local ingredients to the complete result, using the bridge for the connecting explanation.
3. Consult the [scientific route](../science/README.md), [theorem](../science/simulation_theorem.md) and [verification guide](../verification/README.md) for proof details and reproducible finite checks.

The learning material describes the shared scientific story. Its targets and scope are independent of any individual reader's assessment history.
